How to play

On a phone

Hold your phone sideways. Tilt it like a steering wheel to steer, or put your left thumb on the wheel and slide it left or right. Your right thumb works the pedals, and you can slide it from one button to the next. DRIFT and NITRO keep the gas on, so you never have to hold two buttons with one thumb. Change Steering or turn on Auto gas on the title screen.

On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to play full screen without the Safari bars.

Police Chase

Smash things in the street for points. Hits less than 2.5 s apart build a combo, up to ×5. At 8,000 points the Golden Eagle appears under a gold light beam. Drive through it to win. Smashing raises your heat and brings more police. Get out of their sight for 7 s to shake them off for a bonus. If they box you in and you stop, you're busted.

Coast Race

Race five rivals around the coast road and finish first. Tuck in behind a rival for a slipstream tow. Press gas just as the "1" fades for a perfect start.

Drifting

Steer into a corner and hold Space (DRIFT on a phone) to slide. Drifting fills nitro, and the tyre sparks change colour as the drift charges: blue after 0.6 s, orange after 1.5 s, purple after 2.6 s. Let go to fire a Mini, Super or Ultra Turbo. Steer against the slide to widen it, or into it to tighten it.

Gamepads work too: RT gas, LT brake, A nitro, B drift.
