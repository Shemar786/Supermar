Police Chase

Smash things in the street for points. Hits less than 2.5 s apart build a combo, up to ×5. At 8,000 points the Golden Eagle appears under a gold light beam. Drive through it to win. Smashing raises your heat and brings more police. Get out of their sight for 7 s to shake them off for a bonus. If they box you in and you stop, you're busted.

Coast Race

Race five rivals around the coast road and finish first. Hold Space through a corner to drift. Drifting fills nitro, and letting go after 0.7 s fires a mini turbo. Tuck in behind a rival for a slipstream tow. Press gas just as the "1" fades for a perfect start.

Gamepads work too: RT gas, LT brake, A nitro, B drift.
